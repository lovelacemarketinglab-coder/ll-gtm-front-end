# V1 site foundation

## Purpose

Give a Southern Oregon small-business owner enough clarity and confidence to respond to outreach and consider a tightly scoped, one-time marketing service.

## Public routes

- `/` — positioning, current offer, practical working style, contact path
- `/services` — scope, fit, exclusions, process, and turnaround
- `/about` — grounded studio positioning without inflated credentials
- `/contact` — intentionally non-operational inquiry form pending an approved email/form connection

## Component structure

- `SiteHeader` and `SiteFooter` provide the shared shell.
- `PageIntro` standardizes interior-page hierarchy.
- `ArrowIcon` is a small reusable interface mark.
- Service content stays in the Services page until more than one validated offer warrants a data model.

## Visual system

- Warm off-white canvas and near-black typography
- Restrained cobalt-violet accent
- Editorial serif display paired with a clean sans-serif body
- Fine rules, modest radii, generous whitespace, and no stock imagery
- A numbered “field note” motif adds a specific studio character without implying scale

## Content guardrails

- No prices until explicitly approved for publication.
- No rankings, guaranteed outcomes, fabricated proof, team size, or client claims.
- AI is described as an assistive working method; deliverables receive human review.
- Private operating-repository material is never imported wholesale.

## Deferred decisions

- Real inquiry destination and privacy language
- Whether and how the pilot price appears publicly
- Final logo/wordmark asset
- Domain, deployment, analytics, and any form backend
